---
name: claim-reduction
description: Consolidate a research programme into one or two essential claims and the minimum supporting mathematics and experiments.
---
Read the active season, claims, and evidence. Identify the central distinction, weakest load-bearing assumption, smallest proof spine, and minimal discriminating experiment. Classify each extra lemma or experiment as essential, diagnostic, redundant, or future work with reasons. Preserve negative evidence and scope limitations. Propose removals without deleting artifacts or changing the human's scientific target. Return a short proposed paper spine with explicit unresolved obligations.
