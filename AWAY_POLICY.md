# Research while the human is away

Coordinators may run seven days a week. Human review and education delivery occur only on weekday mornings. On weekends, save progress and pending decisions for Monday.

Each human-launched goal must record its target, owner, permitted actions, GPU/time/cost budget, job IDs, expected artifacts, checkpoint path, retry allowance, fallback ladder, and stop conditions. Missing authority means park the affected action until the next review. Resume independent authorized work.

Before retrying a job, inspect live scheduler identity and partial artifacts. Unknown status never permits a duplicate submission. Retry infrastructure failures only within the recorded allowance and remaining budget; preserve the scientific protocol.

The default response to uncertainty is a durable checkpoint and deferred decision. Protect uninterrupted paper reading, mathematics, and creative thought.

An executor must remain online for local supervision. Scheduling prompts alone cannot provide GPU recovery while the executor is asleep. Run recovery through a durable queue with restart reattachment, unique task keys, and bounded retries.

Understanding reports may explain provisional findings when clearly labeled. Formal verification status remains separate; a coordinator may not grant it merely to enable teaching.

Notification limitation: the desktop scheduler's available quiet setting still permits failure notifications. Complete absence of interruptions requires muting the app or using system Focus during protected time.
