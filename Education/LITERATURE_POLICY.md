# Literature-grounded understanding policy

Education artifacts must connect new findings to durable human intellectual lineages, not merely to agent-generated explanations.

## Source priority

1. Foundational papers, monographs, and textbooks written before 2023.
2. Authoritative surveys or lecture notes by established experts.
3. Post-2023 work only when the author or institution is explicitly trusted by the human, or when the source is indispensable to the exact new result. State why the exception qualifies.
4. Agent prose is synthesis, never the primary authority for a concept.

Prefer original sources for the central theorem or method and a textbook or high-quality exposition for understanding. For probability and learning theory, seek the foundational lineage behind tools such as concentration inequalities, matrix concentration, empirical processes, kernel methods, and the neural tangent kernel rather than citing only recent applications. “Tropp inequality” should be resolved to the exact named result and source rather than used as an ambiguous label.

## What every study brief must contain

- An intellectual lineage: which earlier human idea this descends from and what changed.
- At least one foundational paper or textbook citation with a stable link, DOI, arXiv identifier, or bibliographic entry.
- A short, legally compliant excerpt when it materially improves understanding; otherwise a precise paraphrase with page, section, or theorem location.
- A “read deeper” path of at most three items: start with the most illuminating human exposition, then the original source, then an optional advanced treatment.
- A clear separation between what the cited source establishes, what the present research establishes, and the education agent's interpretation.

Do not fabricate quotations, page numbers, theorem numbers, or citations. Read the relevant section before quoting or summarizing it. Respect quotation limits and link to the source. If source access is unavailable, mark the literature connection provisional and omit direct quotations.

## Human trust preferences

The human maintains `Education/TRUSTED_SOURCES.md`: trusted post-2023 authors, institutions, textbooks, and preferred expositors. Absence from that file is not a negative judgment; it means post-2023 material requires explicit justification before entering a brief.
