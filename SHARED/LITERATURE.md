# Living literature review

Agents append entries as they learn things; the human prunes and promotes. An entry an agent adds is marked `[agent]`; after human review it becomes `[reviewed]`.
Keep entries short. Link to the task that found it. Never summarise a paper you have not actually read the relevant section of.
